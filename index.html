BeThere — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

BeThere (“the app”) is made by Portordevs (“we”). BeThere helps you make time for the people who matter. It is built to keep your information on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with information, in plain words.

The short version

  • There is no BeThere account, no advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • The people, plans, notes, moments and photos you add are stored on your phone. We don't have servers that receive them, and we can't see them.
  • Your phone's built-in AI (where your phone has one) runs on your phone.
  • Information leaves your phone only if you choose to: for example if you connect an online AI service, save a backup file, add a plan to your calendar, share something, or buy Premium through Google Play.

Information you add

You can add people (name, relationship, optional phone number, email and photo), important dates, plans and their notes, things to remember, moments with photos, Be Present and Me Time sessions, and a little about yourself (your name, photo and working hours). All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Contacts

When you add someone from your contacts, you pick one contact with Android's contact picker and BeThere copies that person's name, number and photo. If you allow the optional Contacts permission, BeThere reads only the people you have linked, to keep their number and photo up to date. Your contacts are never uploaded or shared.

Photos and camera

Photos you choose or take with the camera are copied into BeThere's private storage on your phone. BeThere does not read the rest of your photo library.

AI features

OptionWhat happens to your information
Your phone's AI (default, on supported phones)BeThere uses Gemini Nano through Android's AICore, which runs on your phone. What BeThere asks it stays on your phone. Google's ML Kit software, which BeThere uses to talk to it, may send Google basic diagnostic and usage information (such as device and app details and performance), as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure.
BeThere's built-in suggestions (phones without built-in AI, or when AI is off)Worked out entirely on your phone.
Online AI you choose: Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Gemini online (Google)Only if you choose one in Settings and add your own key. BeThere then sends that company what the AI needs to help: first names as you saved them, relationships, how often you like to connect, days since you last connected, upcoming plans and their notes, birthdays and dates, things to remember, your working hours and the time of day. Phone numbers, email addresses, photos and contacts are never sent. That company's own privacy policy and terms apply. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Your key is stored encrypted on your phone and is never included in backups. You can remove it at any time.

Notifications, widget and calendar

Reminders and the home-screen widget are created on your phone. If you add a plan to your calendar, BeThere hands it to your calendar app, which may sync it according to its own settings.

Calls and messages

When you call, message or video call someone from BeThere, the app opens the app you choose (for example your phone, WhatsApp or Signal). BeThere does not read your calls or messages.

BeThere Premium and payments

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment, and BeThere never sees your payment details. The app receives only whether you own Premium, and remembers that on your phone. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play purchases.

Backups

BeThere is excluded from cloud backups. You can save your own backup file with Settings › Data & Storage; it goes only where you choose to save it. Android's phone-to-phone transfer can also move BeThere to a new phone.

App lock

App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock. BeThere never receives your biometric data or screen lock.

Children

BeThere is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete anything in the app at any time, switch AI features off, remove an AI key, and delete all data by uninstalling. Because we do not hold your information, we have nothing to give you access to or delete on our side. If you use an online AI service, contact that company about information sent to it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
